DataFast versus Fathom is the comparison to read when both products claim they can show revenue and you want the invoice and the privacy story to be specific. Fathom is a cookieless website-analytics product with a published entry price and a lot of sites in that price. DataFast is a revenue-attribution product with a lower entry price, one site on Starter, and a default script that uses cookies.

Figures below were re-read on 25 November 2026. We did not subscribe to either product for this page. DataFast means the analytics app at datafa.st. It does not mean the Arizona IT company or the Ecuador payments company. That disambiguation is in the [review](/reviews/datafast/).

## The entry invoices

Fathom’s pricing title and meta description say plans start at $15 a month for up to 100,000 monthly pageviews and 50 sites. On the page, Standard is described as everything you need to measure your sites, priced at $15 a month on the card we read. Business is $39 a month and says it is for teams and for companies that build on the analytics through the API or AI reporting. The Business checkout link at that card is labeled `business_monthly_100k`. The page says everything in Standard is included, plus 10 team seats besides you, roles, a team audit log, and higher API limits. A FAQ says traffic from 100,000 to 25 million pageviews does not by itself force a move from Standard to Business. Another FAQ says there is no free plan. Extra site packs of 50 are $10 a month or $100 a year. The pageview slider continues upward. Higher dollar steps were not copied, because the static price we could tie to a named plan at the entry tier is the $15 and $39 pair.

DataFast Starter is $9 a month at 10,000 events and $19 at 100,000. Growth is $19 and $39 at those steps. Starter is 1 website and 1 team member. Growth is 30 websites and 30 team members. The [pricing page](/pricing/datafast/) has the full event ladder through 10 million plus. At 100,000 events, DataFast Starter is $19 against Fathom Standard at $15, and Fathom includes 50 sites at that $15. DataFast is the lower invoice only while you are on a small event step with one site. A client roster reverses it.

## Revenue and cookies

Fathom’s pricing page lists event and revenue tracking, UTM campaigns, and “no cookie banners required” as part of the product, not as a Business upsell. That is the practical difference from Plausible, which points revenue tracking at Business.

DataFast’s homepage is the revenue pitch: connect a payment processor and see which channel brought paying customers. The homepage FAQ names Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, and Shopify, plus a Payment API. The default script uses cookies, and the FAQ says you may need a banner for it. The cookieless script is the one that skips a cookie for the visitor id, with a salt that rotates about every 24 hours and weaker long-journey attribution. If “no banner” is the requirement, Fathom’s positioning matches it more directly than DataFast’s default install. If you use DataFast cookieless to chase the same requirement, read DataFast’s accuracy note first. The [cookieless guide](/guides/cookieless-analytics/) quotes that trade.

We are not claiming one revenue report is more accurate. The pages describe different setups. Fathom says revenue tracking is included. DataFast documents provider connections and an attribution guide, and it warns that cookieless mode hurts that attribution. A test payment is the check. A paragraph is not.

## Which one to start

Start Fathom if you have many sites, you want the cookieless pitch as the default, and revenue tracking on the entry plan is enough of a description for you. The 7-day trial is what the pricing meta description advertises. Confirm the card requirement in the signup flow. We did not see it stated in the HTML.

Start DataFast if the site count is one, the question is channel revenue, and you are willing to connect a provider and choose the cookie script deliberately. The 14-day trial is in the terms, and the homepage says no card is required. Do not buy DataFast as a 50-site agency plan. That is the Fathom entry price’s job, or DataFast Growth if you have already accepted a higher event tier and a 30-site cap.
