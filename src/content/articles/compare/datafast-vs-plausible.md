DataFast vs Plausible is a choice between a revenue product and a cookieless traffic product that can add revenue on a higher plan. They stop being interchangeable as soon as you name the report you open.

Figures below are as of 26 November 2026. DataFast here is [datafa.st](https://datafa.st/), Marc Lou's analytics product. The [review](/reviews/datafast/) separates it from the other companies that share the name.

## What DataFast vs Plausible is actually choosing

Plausible is a privacy-first analytics product: no cookies, hosted in the EU, with a one-page traffic dashboard. Starter is one site for solo use. Growth is up to 3 sites and 3 team members, with sharing. Business is the plan for funnels, journeys, revenue tracking, custom properties, the Stats API, and Data Studio. The trial is 30 days and does not require a card. Yearly billing is ten times the monthly price.

DataFast is organized around which channel brings paying customers. The default script uses cookies. A cookieless script exists, and the visitor salt rotates about every 24 hours, so long revenue journeys are weaker. Documented payment connections include Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, and Shopify, plus a Payment API. The trial is 14 days and does not require a card. The terms do not describe an ongoing free tier.

If the sentence you care about is "no cookie for the analytics script," Plausible leads with that. If the sentence is "which source brought the payment," DataFast is built around that join.

## Price, with the units kept separate

As of 26 November 2026, Plausible lists monthly USD prices from 10,000 through 10 million pageviews. At 10,000: Starter $9, Growth $14, Business $19. At 100,000: $19, $29, and $39.

DataFast lists Starter at $9 and Growth at $19. Both figures are published starting prices for 10,000 monthly events. At 100,000 events the prices are $19 and $39. The full ladder is on the [pricing page](/pricing/datafast/).

A store that sends a payment event and a custom goal will consume DataFast events faster than Plausible pageviews, on the same visits. Compare the unit, then the feature.

On DataFast, connecting a documented provider is how you get the report the product is sold on. On Plausible, revenue tracking is on Business. At 10,000 pageviews, Plausible Business is $19. DataFast Growth is $19 at 10,000 events. Both figures match. The plans do not. DataFast Growth is the 30-site, 30-seat plan with social mentions. Plausible Business is the revenue and funnel plan.

## When to keep Plausible

Keep Plausible if the one-page traffic dashboard is what you wanted, if EU hosting matters to you, or if Business is acceptable and you do not want a second script. Fathom is the closer cookieless neighbor if you want revenue tracking on the entry plan and more sites in the base price. That matchup is [DataFast vs Fathom](/compare/datafast-vs-fathom/).

Keep DataFast if you will connect payments and you want that join on the entry analytics plan. Do not buy either one as a product analytics suite.
