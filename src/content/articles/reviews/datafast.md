DataFast is a web analytics tool for seeing which marketing channels bring in paying customers. This review covers the product at [datafa.st](https://datafa.st/). It is a fit when you will connect payments. It is a poor fit when you need a product suite or a Google Ads report. The plan ladder is on the [DataFast pricing](/pricing/datafast/) page.

Some links in this review are affiliate links. If you subscribe through one of them, MetricsPick may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Details are from public product information as of 26 November 2026. There is no star rating.

## What is DataFast?

DataFast is web analytics aimed at founders. The terms, effective 15 February 2026, describe dashboards for pageviews, custom goals, and payment events. You install a script and connect a payment provider to see revenue next to traffic. The homepage calls this revenue-first analytics.

The data processing agreement names JustShipIt Pte. Ltd. as the vendor trading as DataFast. It was last updated on 9 August 2025. The customer is the controller and DataFast is the processor. Singapore law governs the agreement.

## Other companies that use the DataFast name

Two other businesses use the name. Neither is this product, and neither has an affiliate relationship with MetricsPick.

DataFast Networks provides managed IT in Arizona and lists an address in Peoria. Its public history is an IT company, not a web analytics subscription. Its hourly plans are separate from the prices in this review.

Datafast S.A. is an electronic payments company at datafast.com.ec, established in Quito. Connecting Stripe to datafa.st does not involve that company.

## Who created DataFast?

Marc Lou identifies himself as the creator on the homepage. The footer credits him. The company named in the data processing agreement is JustShipIt Pte. Ltd., trading as DataFast. MetricsPick has no other relationship with Lou. Claims on the homepage about his other products are his marketing, not measurements from this site.

## DataFast features

**Web analytics.** The terms count a pageview, a payment, a free-trial signup, or a custom goal as one event. Events share one monthly allowance across every site on the account.

**Revenue attribution.** Native connections listed in the docs include Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, and Shopify. Paddle, WooCommerce, and other providers appear in the docs as well. You can also send payments through the API. The report is which source, campaign, or page showed up on a paying customer.

**Goals and funnels.** You can track signups or other actions and follow a path to a conversion. The [API guide](/guides/datafast-api/) covers sending those events from a server.

**Plan features at 10,000 events.** Both Starter and Growth include the API, CLI, and MCP, plus 100,000 AI bot requests per billing cycle. Growth includes social mentions and X link attribution. Starter does not. The [MCP guide](/guides/datafast-mcp/) is the assistant setup. The [alternatives](/alternatives/datafast/) page is the shortlist if you want a different product.

The default script uses cookies. The cookieless script hashes a visitor id from the IP address, user agent, domain, and a salt that rotates about every 24 hours. That makes longer customer journeys harder to attribute. The [cookieless guide](/guides/cookieless-analytics/) explains the tradeoff. You may still need a cookie banner for the default script, depending on your setup.

## DataFast pricing at a glance

Starter is from $9 a month and Growth is from $19 a month. Both figures are published starting prices for 10,000 monthly events. At the top published step, Starter is $199 and Growth is $399. Yearly billing is ten times the monthly price. A year of Starter at 10,000 events is $90. Extra bot-traffic capacity is $9 per extra million requests a month.

The [pricing guide](/pricing/datafast/) has every tier, the trial, and the event cap. A separate AI visibility product has its own prices and is not part of this review.

The 14-day trial does not require a card. If you do not subscribe, DataFast locks the dashboard, stops tracking, and deletes the data seven days after the trial ends. If you cancel a paid plan, tracking continues for 30 days, then the data is deleted.

At about 75 percent of the event limit, you get an email. At 100 percent, the dashboard locks until you upgrade, and tracking continues. The plan cards list 1 team member on Starter and 30 on Growth. One homepage FAQ says team members are unlimited. The cards do not. Check the card for the tier you would buy.

## Who should try DataFast?

The trial is worth using if the site already gets traffic, you use a documented payment provider, and you have a specific attribution question. Send a real or test payment and see whether the source you expect gets the credit. Use the default script if your setup allows cookies and the sale can happen days later.

If you need session replay, you will not connect payments, or you want 50 client sites on the lowest plan, use the comparisons on this page. If you came here for the Arizona IT company or the Ecuador payments company, their sites are in the sources.
