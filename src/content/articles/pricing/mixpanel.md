Mixpanel prices events, not seats. As of 26 November 2026, Free, Growth, and Enterprise include unlimited seats. The number that moves the invoice is how many events the organization ingests. Pageviews and product actions you choose to send count. If you estimate from a GA4 session number, you are estimating the wrong unit until you decide which actions become events.

## Free, and the line after it

Free includes up to 1 million events a month, 10,000 session replays, up to 1,000 experiment monthly event units, and up to 10 active feature flags. Multi-touch attribution is not on Free. It is on Growth and Enterprise. If your billing screen shows an older plan, confirm that version before you argue with a number from this page.

Read Mixpanel's pricing docs for what happens when a free project passes the event limit. Do not discover it during a launch week.

## Growth packs

Growth's published starting price is $0. The first million events sit inside that base. Yearly billing:

1 million, $0. 1.5 million, $100 a month. 2 million, $185. 3 million, $270. 5 million, $438. 10 million, $840. 20 million, $1,635.

Monthly billing at the same steps: $0, $140, $259, $378, $613, $1,176, and $2,289. Mixpanel also publishes in-between steps. Above 20 million events, contact sales. The table on this page keeps the steps a founder is most likely to hit.

At 1.5 million events, monthly billing is $140 and yearly is $100. Mixpanel's own savings figure for that step is $480 a year. A yearly pack bought for a launch that then quiets down is the expensive version of a tool you are not opening.

## The startup year

Early-stage companies founded less than 5 years ago, with up to $8 million in total funding, that have not redeemed another offer, can get a first year on the Startup Plan. Read the startup terms before you count that year in a budget. It is not the same offer as Amplitude's under-20-employees, under-$10-million year of Growth.

## Comparing the first paid dollar

At 1 million events, Mixpanel, PostHog, and Amplitude can all be $0, and Amplitude can stay $0 until 2 million. At 1.5 million, Mixpanel prints $100 a month on yearly billing. PostHog's anonymous card prices the extra 500,000 events at $0.00005, which is $25, if you send anonymous events and nothing else. The [PostHog](/pricing/posthog/) and [Amplitude](/pricing/amplitude/) guides hold the detail. The [alternatives](/alternatives/mixpanel/) page tells you when not to switch.

## If the price you want is a marketing price

Mixpanel's packs are product-analytics prices. A content site that only needs referrers should not buy 1.5 million product events to see them. Plausible and Fathom publish pageview prices. GA4 is free. DataFast publishes Starter at $9 a month for 10,000 events when the report is revenue by channel. The [pricing page](/pricing/datafast/) is that ladder. [DataFast vs Mixpanel](/compare/datafast-vs-mixpanel/) is the split. The [review](/reviews/datafast/) explains the cap. The DataFast link is an affiliate link.

If you are under 1 million events and the free reports cover what you open, the correct Mixpanel price is $0. Pay when you are close to the cap or when a Growth-only line, such as multi-touch attribution, is the reason the team is blocked.
