DataFast pricing is a volume ladder, not a seat fee. As of 26 November 2026, the homepage lists monthly USD prices for Starter and Growth at nine event steps. The numbers in the table above are those prices.

This page is the analytics plans for the product at datafa.st, Marc Lou's web analytics tool. It is not a price list for DataFast Networks in Arizona or for Datafast S.A. in Ecuador. The [review](/reviews/datafast/) separates those names. If the price is right and the product is not, the [alternatives](/alternatives/datafast/) page is the shortlist. The [Plausible](/compare/datafast-vs-plausible/) and [GA4](/compare/datafast-vs-google-analytics/) comparisons are the two closest forks. Links to datafa.st on this page are affiliate links.

## The monthly ladder

An event, in the terms effective 15 February 2026, is any point the script tracks: pageviews, payment events, free-trial signups, and custom goals. They count equally, across every website on the account, inside the monthly billing cycle. A yearly subscription is still metered every month.

The steps are 10,000, 100,000, 200,000, 500,000, 1 million, 2 million, 5 million, 10 million, and 10 million plus.

Starter monthly prices: $9, $19, $29, $49, $69, $89, $129, $169, $199.

Growth monthly prices: $19, $39, $59, $99, $139, $179, $259, $339, $399.

## Yearly billing

Yearly prices are ten times the monthly price. The homepage calls that two months free. A 10,000-event Starter year is $90, against twelve months at $9 ($108). A 10,000-event Growth year is $190, against $228. Multiply any monthly cell by ten if you pay yearly.

## What the plan cards include

The ticks below are from the 10,000-event cards. Website, team-member, and social-mention limits are set on the plan type, not on the event step.

Both cards include the event allowance, 100,000 AI bot requests, and API, CLI, and MCP. Extra bot capacity is $9 a month per additional million requests. That meter is separate from normal analytics.

Starter is 1 website, 1 team member, and 3 years of data retention. Social media mentions and X link attribution are off.

Growth is 30 websites, 30 team members, and 5+ years of data retention, plus social mentions and X link attribution.

One homepage FAQ lists unlimited team members. The cards list 1 and 30. Treat the card on the tier you are about to pay for as the limit.

## Trial, lock, and cancellation

New users get a 14-day trial. The homepage button says $0.00 due today and no card required. If you do not subscribe, the dashboard locks, tracking stops, and analytics data is deleted 7 days after the trial ends. If you cancel a paid subscription, tracking continues for 30 days and is then deleted.

At about 75 percent of the event limit, DataFast emails you. At 100 percent, the dashboard locks until you upgrade to a plan that covers usage in that cycle. Events continue to be tracked. You cannot select a plan with a lower limit than the usage you already have.

The [review](/reviews/datafast/) covers what the product is for, and when the price is the wrong question because you need replay, flags, or Google Ads.
