As of 26 November 2026, Amplitude's plans are Free, Plus, Growth, and Enterprise. Every plan includes unlimited seats. The meter that matters is events.

## The free plan

Free includes 2 million events per month, no credit card, and no time limit. The included list names product analytics, one-time cohort syncs, 2,000 AI feedback records, 10,000 monthly session replays, and limited experiments, guides, and surveys. The pricing page also lists unlimited feature flags. Free retention is one year. Custom behavioral cohorts require Plus or higher.

If your volume is under 2 million events and you can work inside those limits, the price is $0. The [alternatives guide](/alternatives/amplitude/) is for when the product shape is wrong, not for saving money below that line.

## Plus has a $0 starting price

Plus is the self-serve paid tier. The published starting price is $0. The first 2 million events are free. A credit card is required. It scales to 70 million events. The added list is custom events, 20 behavioral cohorts, alerts, heatmaps, and two years of retention.

There is no single published Plus price at 5 million events. If the decision needs that figure, run the estimator and save the result. Renewal can be monthly or annual. Amplitude does not publish a monthly-versus-annual discount you can compare with Mixpanel's pack table. The estimator or the contract is the source.

## Growth, Enterprise, and the startup year

Growth and Enterprise are quotes. The startup offer is specific: companies with fewer than 20 employees and less than $10 million raised can get one year of Growth at no cost, with no card for that year, metered at up to 100 million events or 200,000 monthly tracked users. Apply on Amplitude's site. This site has no role in the approval.

Mixpanel's startup rules are different: founded less than 5 years ago and up to $8 million in funding. Do not fill out one form and assume the other.

## A dollar next to PostHog and Mixpanel

PostHog publishes a rate after a free million events. Mixpanel publishes a pack: 1.5 million events is $100 a month on the yearly column. Amplitude stays free through 2 million events and then asks the estimator. For a team at 500,000 events, all three can be $0. For a team at 3 million events, Amplitude Free is no longer enough. The [PostHog pricing](/pricing/posthog/) and [Mixpanel pricing](/pricing/mixpanel/) pages can be compared with each other at a stated volume. Compare them with Amplitude where Amplitude is clearly free, and use the estimator everywhere else.

## When the Amplitude price is the wrong topic

If the report you need is revenue by marketing channel, Amplitude's event meter is a costly way to store pageviews. DataFast publishes Starter at $9 a month for 10,000 events and is built for that report. It will not give you 20 behavioral cohorts. GA4 is free if the channel is Google Ads. Plausible and Fathom publish site-analytics prices.

Pay Amplitude when a person on the team will use product analytics. The revenue path is a different price: the [DataFast pricing](/pricing/datafast/) ladder and the [DataFast vs Amplitude](/compare/datafast-vs-amplitude/) comparison. The DataFast link is an affiliate link. It is not an Amplitude coupon.

Before you add a card, save three numbers: monthly events, monthly replay recordings, and whether you need more than the free cohort behavior. Free includes 10,000 replays. If you are under that and under 2 million events, the card is optional. Custom events, saved behavioral cohorts, alerts, and heatmaps are on Plus.
