Fathom Analytics alternatives are worth a look when the simple dashboard is fine and the commercial terms are not: no free plan, a card for the trial, or an entry price built for many sites. If you use those sites and you want revenue included, staying is coherent.

Prices below are as of 26 November 2026.

## Fathom Analytics alternatives at a glance

Fathom's published starting price is $15 a month for up to 100,000 pageviews and 50 sites. Business at that step is $39. Both figures are published prices for that step. Every plan includes the same analytics features, including event and revenue tracking. Extra seats are $10 a month or $100 a year. Extra site packs of 50 are the same. The table above continues through 25 million pageviews. Custom plans sit above that.

## Plausible

Plausible is from $9 a month for 10,000 pageviews. Growth at that step is $14 and Business is $19. Revenue tracking is on Business, not on every plan. Growth includes up to 3 sites, not 50. Choose Plausible if one or a few sites and a $9 traffic plan matter more than Fathom's site bundle. The [Plausible alternatives](/alternatives/plausible/) page has the ladder. The [cookieless guide](/guides/cookieless-analytics/) covers both scripts.

## DataFast when revenue is why you are shopping

DataFast is from $9 a month for 10,000 events. It is built to show which source brought a payment, once you connect a provider. Starter is one website. You give up Fathom's 50-site entry plan and its cookieless default, unless you accept DataFast's cookieless mode and its shorter memory. The comparison is [DataFast vs Fathom](/compare/datafast-vs-fathom/). The [review](/reviews/datafast/) covers the limits. The button on this page is an affiliate link.

## GA4 and PostHog

GA4 is free and uses cookies. It is the alternative when you want to stop paying and you can live in Google's reports. The [GA4 alternatives](/alternatives/google-analytics/) guide is the wider map.

PostHog is the alternative when "simple" stopped being enough and you need replay, flags, or product funnels. The first million analytics events are free. The [pricing notes](/pricing/posthog/) show the rates after that. A simple client dashboard and a product suite are different purchases. The [attribution guide](/guides/marketing-attribution-tools/) helps if the real question is which visit gets credit.
