Amplitude alternatives depend on why you are leaving. Price, complexity, and "we never needed product analytics" are three different shortlists. Amplitude is a product-analytics platform with a large free event tier.

Plans below are as of 26 November 2026.

## Leave the category, or stay in it

Free includes 2 million events a month, no card, and no time limit. If that covers you, stay. Plus has a published starting price of $0, requires a card, and scales to 70 million events. There is no single public dollar rate for every event after the free allowance. The [pricing page](/pricing/amplitude/) is where to run the estimator.

Startups with fewer than 20 employees and less than $10 million raised can apply for one year of Growth at no cost. Do not assume a Mixpanel startup form covers Amplitude. The rules differ.

## PostHog as the suite-shaped alternative

[PostHog](/alternatives/posthog/) lets you multiply. After a free million events, anonymous events have a published rate. The extra 500,000 events at $0.00005, on top of the free million, are $25 before other products. You also get replay and flags in the same suite. Choose PostHog when you want that rate in writing and you will use more than analytics. The [PostHog pricing](/pricing/posthog/) guide has the card.

## Mixpanel as the event-analytics alternative

[Mixpanel](/alternatives/mixpanel/) prints packs. At 1.5 million events, yearly billing is $100 a month and monthly billing is $140. Both figures are published prices for that step. Choose Mixpanel when you want a pack table instead of an estimator, and you are ready to compare feature rows at that volume. The [Mixpanel pricing](/pricing/mixpanel/) guide holds the higher steps.

## DataFast if you did not need Amplitude

DataFast is from $9 a month for 10,000 events. It is built for which channel brought a payment. It will not give you behavioral cohorts, replay, or Amplitude's experiment tools. Choose it when the events you were about to send were marketing visits and payments. The comparison is [DataFast vs Amplitude](/compare/datafast-vs-amplitude/). The [review](/reviews/datafast/) is the product note. The link is an affiliate link.

## GA4, Plausible, and Fathom for the marketing site

GA4 is free if the channel is Google Ads. [Plausible](/guides/cookieless-analytics/) and Fathom publish site-analytics prices and do not ask you to design a tracking plan first. The [Google Analytics alternatives](/alternatives/google-analytics/) guide groups them. Use those when Amplitude's event meter would be a costly way to store pageviews.

## How to choose

Pay Amplitude when someone will use product analytics. The free tier is large enough that an alternative can wait until the event chart or a missing Plus feature forces it. If the estimator comes back higher than PostHog's card or Mixpanel's pack at the same volume, those two are the comparisons. If the events were pageviews, leave the category.
