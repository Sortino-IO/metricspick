A DataFast alternative is useful only if you name what you are leaving. DataFast is a revenue-attribution tool at [datafa.st](https://datafa.st/). Plausible, Fathom, GA4, PostHog, Simple Analytics, and Umami answer different questions. Sometimes the sensible move is to stay.

Prices for DataFast on this page are as of 26 November 2026. The [review](/reviews/datafast/) separates this product from the Arizona IT company and the Ecuador payments company that share the name.

## When staying is the alternative

Stay if the weekly report is channel, campaign, or page revenue and a documented provider is connected. Starter is $9 a month and Growth is $19 a month. Both figures are published starting prices for 10,000 events. The [pricing page](/pricing/datafast/) has the rest of the ladder. The [API guide](/guides/datafast-api/) is how custom events and revenue get in if you are staying.

## Plausible and Fathom, for the traffic dashboard

[Plausible](/compare/datafast-vs-plausible/) is cookieless traffic. Starter is $9 a month for 10,000 pageviews. Revenue tracking is on Business. [Fathom](/compare/datafast-vs-fathom/) is from $15 a month for up to 100,000 pageviews and 50 sites, with revenue listed on every plan. Choose them when the dashboard is the product and DataFast's payment join is more than you will set up.

## GA4, when the alternative should be free

[Google Analytics](/compare/datafast-vs-google-analytics/) is free in its standard product and tied to Google Ads. It uses cookies. It is the practical alternative when the ad account is how you judge spend, or when you will not pay for analytics. It is a weak alternative if the reason you liked DataFast was a short path from visit to payment. The wider map is the [GA4 alternatives](/alternatives/google-analytics/) guide.

## PostHog, Mixpanel, and Amplitude

These three are alternatives only if you are leaving marketing analytics on purpose. [PostHog](/compare/datafast-vs-posthog/) includes a free allowance of 1 million analytics events, plus replay and flags. [Mixpanel](/compare/datafast-vs-mixpanel/) is free for 1 million events, then published packs. [Amplitude](/compare/datafast-vs-amplitude/) is free for 2 million events and does not publish one dollar rate after that. None of them is a $9 substitute for a channel-revenue screen, and DataFast is not a substitute for their cohorts, replay, or flags.

## Simple Analytics and Umami

[Simple Analytics](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/) is a privacy-friendly traffic dashboard. As of 26 November 2026, its pricing page shows $20 a month at the 100,000 pageview step, a free plan for hobby sites (1 user, 5 websites, 1 month of history), and an extra $20 a month per additional user. It does not publish a DataFast-style payment join.

[Umami](https://umami.is/) is open-source analytics you can self-host. Self-hosting is free. Umami Cloud is the managed service. There is no single public list price for Cloud on the pages checked for this guide. Choose Umami when you want to run the server. Choose DataFast when you want a hosted report of which channel brought the payment.

## How to use the six comparisons

Read one comparison, not all six, unless you are writing the decision down for someone else.

- Revenue versus a cookieless traffic tool: [Plausible](/compare/datafast-vs-plausible/) and [Fathom](/compare/datafast-vs-fathom/).
- Revenue versus the free Google tag: [Google Analytics](/compare/datafast-vs-google-analytics/).
- Revenue versus a product suite: [PostHog](/compare/datafast-vs-posthog/), [Mixpanel](/compare/datafast-vs-mixpanel/), and [Amplitude](/compare/datafast-vs-amplitude/).

DataFast next to GA4, or DataFast next to PostHog, can be a stack with two reports. The [pricing](/pricing/datafast/) page is what the DataFast half costs.
