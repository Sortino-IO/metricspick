### Sources and what was verified

Prices below are monthly USD list prices unless noted. Promotions were ignored. Yearly prices are ten times the monthly price where the vendor says two months free.

**DataFast** (homepage HTML and the page bundle `app/page-72578ce26010208e.js`, plus `/tos` and `/dpa`):

- Starter monthly: $9, $19, $29, $49, $69, $89, $129, $169, $199 at 10k, 100k, 200k, 500k, 1M, 2M, 5M, 10M, and 10M+.
- Growth monthly: $19, $39, $59, $99, $139, $179, $259, $339, $399 at the same steps.
- Yearly price is 10 times monthly (a 10k Starter year is $90, anchor $108).
- 10k cards: 100,000 AI bot requests, API, CLI, and MCP. Extra bot capacity $9 per million requests. Starter is 1 website, 1 team member, 3 years retention. Growth is 30 websites, 30 team members, 5+ years, plus social mentions and X link attribution.
- 14-day trial, no card. Terms effective 15 February 2026. Email around 75 percent of the event limit. Dashboard locks at 100 percent while tracking continues. Data deleted 7 days after an unconverted trial, or 30 days after cancelling a paid plan.
- DPA last updated 9 August 2025. Vendor is JustShipIt Pte. Ltd., trading as DataFast. Singapore law.
- Cookieless doc: visitor salt rotates about every 24 hours (UTC).
- API: `df_` website key and `dft_` account token. `POST /api/v1/goals`. `POST /api/v1/payments` requires amount and currency. A repeated transaction id is idempotent.
- MCP URL `https://datafa.st/api/mcp`. Docs say MCP cannot delete websites or connect and disconnect payment providers.
- A separate AI visibility product is priced on the homepage ($99 and $399 a month in the bundle). It is not in the analytics table.

**Plausible** (homepage slider script and subscription-plans doc): ladder unchanged, from $9 / $14 / $19 at 10k pageviews through $169 / $254 / $339 at 10M.
